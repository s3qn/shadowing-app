Privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-12

Voice recordings

Your recordings are uploaded over HTTPS to our server, transcribed there, and turned into practice sentences.

Account

Sign-in is handled by Supabase. When you sign in with Apple or Google, your email address and an account id are stored there. Apple may provide a private relay address instead of your real one. Deleting your account removes that sign-in account too. If you use the app on one device without signing in (TestFlight build 1), no email is stored.

Your islands

Recordings, transcripts and generated sentences are stored on our server, tied to your account or device.

Third parties

We show no ads, use no tracking SDKs, and do not sell your data. Speech-to-text and the voice run on our own server. To write your practice sentences, the text of your transcript is sent to Anthropic, the company behind Claude, which processes it under its own terms (Anthropic privacy policy). Your audio is not sent to them.

Deletion

In the app, Settings > Delete account removes your islands, practice history and sign-in account right away. You can also email sean@larch-networks.com; email requests are handled within 30 days.
